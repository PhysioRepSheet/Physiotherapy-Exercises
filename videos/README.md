The easy way: open media.html on the website ("Add photos and videos"), pick an exercise and drag your photos or video onto it. It saves the files here and lists them in media.js for you.

By hand: put a video here (e.g. knee-static-quads.mp4), then set `video: "videos/knee-static-quads.mp4"` for that exercise in exercises.js (or add it to media.js).
A little Picture | Video switch then appears for that exercise. Keep videos short and small (under about 25 MB) so they load quickly on phones.
