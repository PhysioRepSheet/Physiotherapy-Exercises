What's changed

New things on RepSheet, newest first.

17 November 2026: copy link, message and home screen

  • "Copy patient page link" button next to Email: one tap copies the link to paste into a text, email or letter.
  • "Message for your patient": a short note (2 or 3 lines) that prints at the top of the handout and shows at the top of the patient page.
  • Patients on a phone get a small "Add to home screen" card, so their exercises open like an app, even without internet. On iPhone it explains how to do it from Safari.
  • Search finds exercises by their other names too. For example "shoulder bridge" finds Bridge, and "superman" finds Arm and leg reach. The exercise keeps its own name on the handout.

17 November 2026: pacing diary

  • New pacing diary for patients: write down activities, how hard they were, and energy and symptoms each day.
  • Limits for each activity, with a timer that says when to stop and rest.
  • A week chart, with a gentle note when a busy day is followed by a crash ("boom and bust").
  • Print a week for the physio, or a blank paper diary. Physios can give starting limits by QR code.
  • The diary is saved only on the patient's phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.

17 November 2026: phones and refreshing

  • Simple and Advanced views: Simple shows just the picture, name, sets, reps, hold and how often for each exercise. Advanced shows everything. The builder remembers your choice.
  • "In this handout": on tablets and computers, a short list of your chosen exercises and their doses stays at the top while you scroll. Tap one to jump to it.
  • "Change several exercises at once": tick the exercises you want, then set sets, reps, hold, how often or weight for all of them in one go.
  • On a phone or tablet, "Make handout PDF" makes a real PDF file, then shows big buttons to save, share or open it.
  • If the page is refreshed by accident, your handout is still there. It's kept in that browser tab only, and wiped when you close the tab.

17 November 2026: a big update

Making handouts

  • Search copes with spelling mistakes, and you can search by what the patient wants to do, such as "stairs" or "getting up from a chair".
  • Pick a body area from a body map, and filter by strength, stretch, balance, movement or breathing.
  • Star your favourite exercises. Your recently used ones are kept too.
  • Look at an exercise before you add it.
  • Set left side, right side or both, and copy an exercise for the other side in one click.
  • Undo after removing an exercise or clearing the handout.
  • Set "How often" for every exercise at once.
  • A gentle warning if a note looks like it has patient details in it.
  • Split a programme into stages, such as "Weeks 0 to 2" and "Weeks 2 to 6".
  • Easy read handouts: one exercise per page, with big pictures.
  • The handout lists what the patient will need, such as a chair or a towel.

On the patient's phone

  • Larger text, easier reading and high contrast options.
  • "How is it going?": patients can say if each exercise feels too easy, about right or too hard, and show it to you.
  • A button to email, call or text you, using the contact details on the handout.
  • Reminders can be set for every day, weekdays, or three days a week.
  • A count of days in a row, and a list of what they will need.
  • A note if the programme is more than 12 weeks old.
  • Patients on a staged programme see one stage at a time.

Other

  • New pages: For NHS teams and Accessibility.
