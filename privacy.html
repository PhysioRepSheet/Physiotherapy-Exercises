Privacy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

In short
  • We don't collect, store or see any patient information.
  • Everything you type stays in your own web browser.
  • There are no accounts, cookies, analytics, tracking or adverts.

About this site

RepSheet is a free tool for physiotherapists to make home exercise handouts. If you have a question about privacy, email [contact email coming soon]. This address is only used for this site.

For physiotherapists using the builder

  • Patient details stay on your computer. The patient's name, the exercises you choose and your notes are held only in the open browser tab. They are never sent to us. So that an accidental refresh doesn't lose your work, the handout is kept in that browser tab only (the browser's "session storage"), and it is wiped as soon as you close the tab or the browser. Press Clear to wipe it straight away.
  • PDFs on phones and tablets: on a phone or tablet, the handout PDF is made on the device itself. Nothing is sent to us. Where it is saved or sent after that is up to you.
  • Your own name and contact details are only remembered if you tick "Remember my name and contact details on this device". They are then saved in your browser on that device only, and you can untick the box at any time to delete them.
  • My templates: if you save a template, its exercises, settings, exercise notes and general advice are saved in your browser on that device only. Templates never include the patient's name, date, goal or plan. You can delete them in "My templates" at any time. "Download backup" saves them to a file on your own computer.
  • My own exercises: if you make your own exercise, its name, steps, starting dose and any photo you add are saved in your browser on that device only. If you use it in a handout, its name and steps (never the photo) go into the patient link and any template link you share. Never put patient details in your own exercises.
  • Favourites and recent exercises: the builder remembers which library exercises you starred or added recently (exercise names only, never anything about a patient), in your browser on that device only.
  • Privacy check: the builder looks for things like NHS numbers, phone numbers, dates of birth and names in boxes that go into the patient link, and warns you. This check runs on your computer. Nothing is sent or saved.
  • Patient tick charts: if the physiotherapist switches on the weekly tick chart, the days a patient ticks on their exercise page are saved in the browser on the patient's own phone only. They are never sent anywhere. Clearing the browser's site data removes them.
  • "How is it going?" check-in, stages and display choices: if a patient answers the check-in, chooses which stage they are on, or picks larger text, easier reading or high contrast, those choices are saved in the browser on their own phone only. They are never sent anywhere. "Copy to send to my physio" only copies the answers; the patient decides whether to paste them into their own message.
  • Contacting the physio: "Email my physio", "Call" and "Text my physio" open the patient's own email, phone or text app using the contact details the physiotherapist typed. Nothing goes through this site.
  • Calendar reminders: if a patient taps "Add a reminder to my calendar", their phone makes a calendar file and opens it in their own calendar app. Nothing is sent to this site. The reminder contains the link to their exercises, which never includes their name. If their calendar syncs online (for example Google or iCloud), the reminder is stored there under their own account.
  • Working offline: your browser keeps a copy of this website's own files (pages, pictures and code) so it still opens without a signal. This copy never includes anything you or a patient typed. Clearing the browser's site data removes it.
  • Printing and PDFs are created by your own browser. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Email opens a new message in your own email program (such as Outlook or NHSmail). The website does not send any email and never sees the address you type.

Patient links and QR codes

  • The link and QR code contain the programme itself: the exercises, sets and reps, notes, ticked progressions, the date, your name and your contact details. The patient's name is never included.
  • The programme sits after the "#" in the link. Web browsers never send that part of a link to the website, so it is not stored on our server or in its logs.
  • Anyone who has the link can open it, in the same way as anyone holding a paper handout can read it. Only share the link or QR code with the patient it is for, and please don't write names, NHS numbers, dates of birth or other identifying details in any notes.

For patients

  • Your exercise page is built inside your own phone or computer from the link your physiotherapist gave you. It isn't stored anywhere.
  • If you want to keep it, save the link or bookmark the page. If you lose it, ask your physiotherapist for a new one.
  • Some exercises may have a video. Videos from YouTube or Vimeo only load if you press "Watch video". We use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode and Vimeo's "do not track" setting. Once a video plays, YouTube's (or Vimeo's) own terms and privacy policy apply to it.

Website hosting

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages (run by GitHub, Inc.). Like any website, the host may keep basic technical logs, such as your device's IP address and which page was requested, to keep the service running and secure. These logs never include the programme in a patient link (see above), and we don't use them to identify anyone.

Feedback and exercise requests

The "Send feedback" and "Request an exercise" links open a Google Form. What you type there is stored by Google and only used to improve this tool. Google's privacy policy applies to the form. Never include patient details in feedback or requests.

Cookies

This site doesn't use cookies, analytics or adverts. The only things it can store on a device are the physiotherapist's own name and contact details, saved templates, their own exercises, favourite and recent exercises (only if they choose to) and, on a patient's phone, the days they have ticked, their check-in answers, which stage they are on and their display choices. To remove them, untick "Remember my name and contact details", delete templates in "My templates", or clear your browser's site data.

Changes

If anything here changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
