Who this site is for

This site is intended for qualified, registered healthcare professionals, such as physiotherapists, to use with their own professional judgement. It is not designed for members of the public to choose exercises for themselves.

Not a substitute for assessment

The exercises, instructions, pictures, suggested sets and reps, progressions and regressions are general examples only. They are not a substitute for a proper clinical assessment, diagnosis or treatment plan, and they may not be suitable for every patient.

Your responsibility as a clinician

  • You are responsible for deciding whether each exercise, its dosage and any progression or regression is safe and suitable for the patient you give it to.
  • Please check every handout before you give it out, including the wording, pictures and any notes.
  • Follow your own organisation's policies, including those on information governance and patient information.

For patients

Only do the exercises your physiotherapist has given you, in the way they explained. Stop any exercise that causes sharp or worsening pain, and contact your physiotherapist or GP. In an emergency, call 999. For urgent advice, call NHS 111.
