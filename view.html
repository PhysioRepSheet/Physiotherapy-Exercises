    How much pain is OK?

    • Green: carry on. A mild ache or stretch that settles within an hour or so.
    • Amber: go easier. Pain that lasts after the exercises, or is worse the next morning. Do fewer, or do them more gently.
    • Red: stop. Sharp pain, or pain that keeps getting worse. Stop and call your physio.
