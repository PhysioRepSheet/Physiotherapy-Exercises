RepSheet

For NHS and clinic teams

Last updated: 17 November 2026

In short
  • RepSheet is a plain website. There are no accounts, no database and no server code.
  • Nothing about a patient is stored by RepSheet or sent to RepSheet.
  • No cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • The physio chooses every exercise and dose, just as with a paper leaflet.

This page is for information governance leads, IT teams and clinical leads who want to know whether RepSheet is safe to use. Please also read our privacy notice and disclaimer.

What RepSheet is

A free website that helps physiotherapists make home exercise handouts. The physio picks exercises, sets how many and how often, then prints the handout, saves it as a PDF, or gives the patient a link or QR code that opens their programme on their own phone.

It is made of ordinary HTML, CSS and JavaScript files. Everything runs inside the browser on the physio's own computer.

What happens to patient information

  • Nothing about a patient is stored or sent to RepSheet. What the physio types stays in their own browser tab. It is kept through an accidental refresh, and wiped when the tab is closed.
  • On phones and tablets the handout PDF is made on the device itself.
  • The patient's name and the date are never put into the patient link or QR code. They only appear on the printed handout.
  • The patient link holds the exercise programme itself, after the # in the address. Browsers never send the part after # to the web server.
  • The builder warns the physio if a note looks like it has a name, NHS number, date of birth, phone number or address in it. It never blocks them, and the check happens on their own computer.
  • Email opens the physio's own email program with the text filled in. RepSheet sends nothing.

What is saved on a device

WhereWhatWhen
Physio's browserTheir own name and contact detailsOnly if they tick "Remember on this device"
Physio's browserTheir templates, own exercises, favourite and recently used exercises (never patient details)When they save or use one
Patient's phoneTicked days, "How is it going?" answers, which stage they are on, and display choices such as larger textOnly when the patient uses those parts

All of this uses the browser's own local storage. It can be cleared at any time by clearing the site's data in the browser.

Clinical responsibility

  • RepSheet does not make clinical decisions. The physiotherapist chooses every exercise and dose and stays responsible for the advice, as with a paper leaflet.
  • The exercises are general examples in plain English. Newly added exercises are marked "Awaiting check" in the builder until a clinician has reviewed them.

Using it offline or on a shared drive

  • Once opened, the builder and patient pages keep working without the internet, and can be installed as an app from the browser menu.
  • For wards with poor wifi, or PCs that block new websites, the whole site can be copied to a shared drive. Open index.html from that folder. Patient links made this way point to the shared drive, so print the handout or use the QR code from the online site instead.
  • The files are public on GitHub, so your IT team can check exactly what the site does.
