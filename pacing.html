        Tips for pacing

        • Do about the same each day. On a good day, don't do lots more. On a bad day, try to do a little.
        • Rest before you get tired, not after.
        • Break big jobs into small parts. For example, hoover one room, then rest.
        • Mix it up. Put an easy job after a hard one.
        • Plan your week. Spread out the busy things.
        • Rest means real rest. Sit or lie down somewhere quiet. Phones and TV are not always restful.

        Watch out for "boom and bust"

        Doing a lot on a good day, then crashing for a few days after, is called boom and bust. Your week chart can help you spot it.

        Doing more

        Only make your limits bigger after talking to your physio. If you have ME/CFS or long COVID, don't push yourself to do more each week. Stay within your energy.

        Get help

        Contact your GP, physio or NHS 111 if you have new symptoms, or you are getting worse. In an emergency, call 999.

        Your diary and your privacy

        Your diary is saved in this web browser, on this phone or computer only. It is not sent to RepSheet or anyone else. If you clear your browser's data, the diary is deleted.

        To share it, print it or show your phone to your physio.

        For physios: give a patient starting limits

        Type the activities and limits you agreed. Your patient scans the QR code, and the limits are added to their diary when they say yes. The link holds only these activities and minutes. Never type anything that identifies the patient.
