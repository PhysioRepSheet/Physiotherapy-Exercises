How it works

  1. 1

    Choose exercises

    Search 50 exercises by name or clinical term, or start from a ready-made template for common conditions.

  2. 2

    Tailor them

    Set sets, reps, holds and how often. Add a weight, a note, and progressions or regressions.

  3. 3

    Hand it over

    Print or save a PDF, email it, or let your patient scan the QR code for moving pictures.

Everything a handout needs, nothing it doesn't

  • Moving pictures

    Clear drawings that move like a GIF on screen and print as numbered steps.

  • A link for the patient

    A QR code opens their programme on any phone. No app, no account, no download.

  • Print the way you need

    Large print, black and white, and a paper saver option. Plain, easy-read wording throughout.

  • Templates

    Ten ready-made starting points, plus your own templates saved on your computer or shared by link.

  • Your own exercises

    Write an exercise that isn't in the library, add a photo, and use it like any other.

  • Copy for notes

    One click copies a clinical summary of what you gave, ready to paste into the patient record.

A handout patients can follow

Each exercise gets step-by-step pictures, short plain-English steps and a big, clear dose. Their goal and plan sit at the top, and a QR code takes them to the moving version.

Nothing about your patient is stored

  • The handout is made in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to us or saved on a server.
  • The patient's name is never put in the link or the QR code.
  • No accounts, cookies, analytics or adverts.
  • Your own name, templates and exercises are kept on your computer only if you choose.
